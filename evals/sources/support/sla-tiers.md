domain: support
title: Support plans and response times
---

Basic plans receive a first response within 24 business hours. Pro plans receive a first response within 8 business hours. Enterprise plans receive a first response within 1 hour at any time, every day of the year. A severity 1 problem means the product is unusable for the whole account and is treated as Enterprise priority on every plan. Response time is measured to the first human reply, not to the automatic acknowledgement.

Business hours for first-response targets are 08:00 to 18:00 on weekdays in the customer's region, excluding public holidays. A ticket that breaches its target is flagged to the team lead and must be answered within 30 minutes. Customers upgrading mid-month are moved to the new tier immediately, and the response targets apply from the moment the upgrade is confirmed in billing.
