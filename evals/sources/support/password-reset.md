title: Resetting a password
---

Customers reset their own password with the Forgot password link on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes and works once. After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. Customers who sign in through single sign-on cannot reset a password here; they must contact their company's identity administrator. Agents must never ask a customer to tell them their password.

If a customer never receives the reset email, the agent first checks the spam folder advice, then confirms the address on the account, and finally asks the customer to wait 10 minutes before requesting another link, because several requests invalidate the older ones. Customers who have lost access to their email address must verify their identity with the account owner or a government identity document before the address can be changed.
