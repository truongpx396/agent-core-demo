title: Invoices and billing details
---

Invoices are generated on the 1st of each month and are available as PDF files under Billing and then Invoices. Only the account owner can change the billing email address. A VAT or tax identification number can be added to the billing profile, and it appears on invoices issued after the change but is not applied retroactively. Failed payments are retried three times over seven days before the account is suspended.

Customers who need an invoice reissued in a different company name can ask for a corrected copy within 30 days of the original date; after that, accounting rules do not allow changes. Credit notes are issued for refunds and appear in the same list as invoices. Enterprise customers can pay by bank transfer with a purchase order number printed on every invoice.
