domain: support
title: Deleting an account
---

Only the account owner can delete an account. Deletion starts a 14-day grace period during which the owner can cancel it by signing in. After the grace period the account is closed and the data stays in encrypted backups for 30 more days before it is permanently purged. Once the data is purged it cannot be recovered by anyone, including support.

Deleting an account cancels all active subscriptions and stops future billing, but fees already paid are not refunded unless the refund policy applies. Team members lose access immediately when the deletion starts, and shared projects owned by the account are removed with it. Customers who want to keep their data should request an export before starting the deletion, because exports are unavailable afterwards.
