---
domain: support
title: Escalating a ticket
---

A Tier-1 agent escalates to Tier-2 when a ticket has not been resolved after 2 hours of work or needs an account-level change. A severity 1 problem skips Tier-2 and pages the on-call engineer directly. Every escalation must include the account name, the steps already tried, the customer's exact error text and the time the customer first reported it. The customer is told who now owns the ticket.

A Tier-2 agent who cannot resolve a ticket within one working day opens an engineering ticket and links it to the customer ticket so progress can be tracked from either side. Engineering fixes are communicated back through the support agent, never directly to the customer. If a customer asks to speak to a manager, the agent offers a call within one business day and records the request in the ticket.
