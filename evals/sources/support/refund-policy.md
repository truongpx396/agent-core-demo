domain: support
title: Refund policy
---

Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of the first purchase. After 30 days, annual plans are refunded pro rata for the unused full months, and monthly plans are not refunded. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Refunds above 1,000 dollars, and any exception to these rules, must be approved by a Tier-2 agent before the customer is told the outcome.

Refunds for add-on services, such as extra storage or premium support, follow the same 30-day window but are calculated from the date the add-on was activated, not from the first purchase. Chargebacks opened with a bank before a refund request are handled by the billing team, not by support agents. When a refund is granted the agent adds the reason code to the ticket so finance can report on it, and sends the customer a confirmation email with the amount.
