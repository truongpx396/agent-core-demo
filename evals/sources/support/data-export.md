domain: support
title: Data export requests
---

An account owner can request a full export of the account's data. Exports are produced as CSV or JSON files and delivered within 72 hours through a secure download link that stays valid for 7 days. The request must come from the verified account owner; an agent cannot approve an export on behalf of a regular user. Large exports above 50 GB are split into several files.

Exports include users, projects, files and activity logs, but not billing card details, which are never stored by the company. Customers on the Pro plan and above can schedule a recurring export every week to their own storage bucket. If an export fails, the customer receives an email with a reference number and the team retries it automatically twice before contacting them.
