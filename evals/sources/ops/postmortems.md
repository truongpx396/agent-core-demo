domain: ops
title: Post-incident reviews
---

Every severity 1 and severity 2 incident gets a blameless postmortem, written within 5 business days. The document gives a timeline, the impact, the root cause and what went well. A review meeting is held and the action items are entered in the tracker, each with one named owner and a due date. A postmortem is not closed until its action items are done or consciously dropped.

A postmortem lists the contributing factors in plain language and avoids naming individuals, because the aim is to improve the system. Detection time and recovery time are recorded for every incident and reported in a monthly review. Action items that would prevent the same class of incident take priority over feature work, and the engineering director reviews overdue items every two weeks.
