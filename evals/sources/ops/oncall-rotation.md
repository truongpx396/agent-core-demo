domain: ops
title: On-call rotation
---

On-call shifts rotate weekly and hand over every Monday at 10:00 UTC. Each shift has a primary and a secondary engineer; the secondary is paged if the primary does not acknowledge within 5 minutes. The outgoing engineer writes a handoff note listing open incidents, noisy alerts and anything unusual. Swaps are arranged in the paging tool at least 24 hours ahead. Engineers on call receive a day of time off after a week with more than 5 pages.

New engineers shadow the on-call for two weeks before joining the rotation and take their first shift as secondary. If an engineer is unreachable during their shift, the manager is paged after 15 minutes and the secondary takes over. Pages outside local working hours are logged so the team can review whether the load is fair. The rotation schedule is published four weeks ahead.
