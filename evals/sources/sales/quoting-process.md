domain: sales
title: Quotes
---

A quote is valid for 30 days from the day it is issued. Quotes use the current price sheet and are created in the CRM, never in a spreadsheet. Any non-standard term, such as custom payment terms or liability caps, needs legal review before the quote is sent. A customer accepts a quote by electronic signature, which creates the order automatically.

A quote shows the term, seat count, unit price, discounts and total in the customer's currency, using the exchange rate set on the first of the month. If the customer requests changes after sending, a new version is created and the old one is marked superseded, so there is only ever one valid quote. Renewal quotes are generated automatically 120 days before the contract end and reviewed by the account owner.
