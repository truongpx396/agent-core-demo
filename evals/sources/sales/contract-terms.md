domain: sales
title: Standard contract terms
---

Contracts are offered for one, two or three years. The standard annual price escalator is 5 percent, and sales may agree to a maximum of 7 percent. Volume pricing steps down at 100 seats and again at 500 seats. Payment terms are net 30 days; net 60 requires finance approval. Auto-renewal is on by default with a 60-day notice window.

Standard terms limit liability to the fees paid in the previous 12 months; any request for a higher cap goes to legal. Customers can add seats at any time at the contracted price, prorated to the end of the term, but reducing seats is only possible at renewal. Data processing terms are included by default for customers in the European Union and signed before the first invoice.
