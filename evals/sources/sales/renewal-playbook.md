domain: sales
title: Renewals
---

Renewal work starts 120 days before the contract ends. The account health score combines product usage, support tickets and executive engagement. At 90 days the rep arranges a meeting with the customer's executive sponsor. Because auto-renewal needs 60 days' notice to cancel, any account still unresolved at 75 days is escalated to the sales manager. Renewals are never discounted without a documented risk.

A renewal quote is sent at 90 days and shows any change in price and in seats. Customers who reduce their seat count are asked for the reason, and the answer is recorded in the CRM loss reason list. Multi-year renewals are encouraged with a one-time discount of up to 5 percent, and the customer success manager joins the renewal call for accounts above 100,000 dollars a year.
