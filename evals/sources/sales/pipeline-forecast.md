domain: sales
title: Pipeline and forecasting
---

Opportunities are forecast in three categories: commit, best case and pipeline. The team holds a forecast call every Monday at 09:00, and reps must update their numbers the evening before. Each stage has a default win probability, and managers may adjust it with a written reason. Healthy pipeline coverage is at least three times the quota for the quarter.

Deals with a close date in the current quarter but no activity in the last 14 days are removed from commit. The forecast is submitted by managers by 17:00 on Monday and is compared with the actual closing totals at quarter end to measure accuracy. New logos and expansions are forecast separately because their conversion rates differ. A forecast miss of more than 10 percent is discussed in the next business review.
