domain: sales
title: Discount approval
---

Account executives may offer a discount of up to 10 percent on their own authority. A discount between 10 and 20 percent needs the sales manager's approval, and anything above 20 percent needs the VP of Sales. No discount may take the deal below the 55 percent gross margin floor. Multi-year commitments may earn an additional 3 percent for each extra year, within the same approval limits.

A discount request must include the customer's budget, the competitor being compared, and the planned close date, and approvers answer within one business day. Discounts are never applied to professional services or training, which are sold at list price. Quarter-end exceptions are not allowed: a deal that needs a discount above the rep's authority after the 25th of the last month needs the VP's approval regardless of size.
