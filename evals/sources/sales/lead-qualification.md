domain: sales
title: Qualifying a lead
---

Leads are scored against BANT: budget, authority, need and timeline. A score of 70 or more marks a hot lead, which must receive a discovery call within 24 hours. A lead with no budget expected within the next 12 months is disqualified and moved to nurture. The score and the reasons are recorded on the lead so another rep can understand the decision.

Inbound leads from the website are routed round-robin to the sales development team and acknowledged by email within 15 minutes. Leads from existing customers are routed to the account owner instead and are never scored as new business. A lead that does not respond after five attempts across two weeks is moved to nurture with a note about the last contact.
