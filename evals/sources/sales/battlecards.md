domain: sales
title: Competitive positioning
---

When a prospect compares us with a competitor, focus on integration depth and total cost, and never disparage the competitor. Our security attestations are SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001, and reports are shared under NDA. Common objections and answers are kept in the battlecard for each competitor; reps should update a card when they hear a new objection three times.

Reference customers are matched by industry and size and must give permission before they are shared; the list of approved references is maintained by marketing. Pricing comparisons are never sent in writing without approval from product marketing. When a prospect asks about a feature that is on the roadmap, reps may describe the direction but must not promise a date.
