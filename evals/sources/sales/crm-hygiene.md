---
domain: sales
title: Keeping the CRM accurate
---

Reps update the opportunity stage within 24 hours of any customer call. Every open opportunity must have a next step with a date. When an opportunity is lost, the loss reason is chosen from the fixed list, not written as free text. Duplicate accounts are merged by sales operations, and reps must not create a new account before searching for an existing one.

The forecast category must match the stage: an opportunity cannot be in commit if no meeting with the economic buyer is recorded. Opportunities with no activity for 30 days are flagged for review and closed as lost after 60 days unless a next step is logged. Notes from calls are saved in the opportunity, not in personal documents, so a colleague can take over without a handover meeting.
