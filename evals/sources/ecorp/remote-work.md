title: Remote work and the home-office stipend
---

Employees become eligible for fully remote work after 90 days with the company and manager approval. During remote work everyone keeps core hours from 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone, when they are expected to be reachable. The company pays a 40 dollar monthly internet stipend and a one-time 300 dollar home-office equipment allowance, claimed through the expense system with the category Remote Setup.

Remote employees must work from the country listed in their contract, because tax and employment rules differ by country; working abroad for more than 14 days needs written approval from People Operations. Teams meet in person at least once per quarter, and travel for these meetings is budgeted separately. Employees whose home internet is unreliable can ask IT for a mobile hotspot instead of the stipend.
