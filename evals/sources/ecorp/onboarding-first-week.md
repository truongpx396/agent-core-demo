title: Onboarding: the first week
---

A laptop is shipped to new hires five days before the start date, and IT sends login details on day one. Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy who is not their manager. Mandatory security training must be completed within 3 days of starting. In the first week the manager shares a 30-60-90 day plan, and the new hire meets each teammate for 20 minutes.

On day one the new hire's accounts for email, chat and the ticketing tool are already created, but access to production systems is granted only after the security training and a signed acceptable use policy. Engineers receive repository access on day two after their buddy reviews the setup guide with them. By the end of the first month the new hire presents what they learned to their team in a 15-minute demo.
