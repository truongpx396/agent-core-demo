domain: ecorp
title: Paid time off
---

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off per year, accrued at 2.08 days per month from the first day of work. Up to 5 unused days carry over into January and anything beyond that expires on 31 January. Time off is requested in Workday; a request longer than 5 consecutive working days needs two weeks of notice. Public holidays are separate and do not use up the allowance.

Sick leave is separate from paid time off: employees have 10 paid sick days per year and need a doctor's note only after 3 consecutive days. Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for either parent, and can be taken in blocks within the first year. Unused time off is paid out when employment ends in countries where the law requires it, and otherwise lapses.
