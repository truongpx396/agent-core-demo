domain: ecorp
title: Expense and reimbursement policy
---

Employees are reimbursed for business expenses that are reasonable and documented. Meals while travelling are capped at 60 dollars per day, and every receipt over 25 dollars must be attached to the expense report. Reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense or they are not reimbursed. A manager can approve a report up to 500 dollars; anything larger goes to Finance for a second approval. Alcohol, personal entertainment and fines are never reimbursable.

Expenses incurred on a company credit card are reconciled monthly by the cardholder, and unreconciled transactions older than 60 days are charged back to the employee's department. Client entertainment needs the client's name and the business purpose written on the receipt. For international trips, amounts are converted at the rate on the day of the charge. Questions about a rejected report go to the Finance helpdesk, which answers within two working days.
