domain: ecorp
title: Booking business travel
---

Book travel through the company travel portal at least 14 days before departure to get the negotiated fares. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed above that. Trains are preferred over flying for trips under 4 hours. Hotels are capped at 220 dollars per night in major cities and 150 dollars elsewhere. Changes after booking go through the portal support line, not directly with the airline.

A traveller who must change a trip within 48 hours of departure can phone the travel agency's emergency line, which is open at all hours. Loyalty points earned on business trips belong to the traveller, but upgrades paid for with points may not be claimed back as expenses. Rental cars are allowed only when public transport is not practical, and the compact class is the default. Per-diem rates for each country are listed in the travel portal.
