title: Performance review cycle
---

Performance is reviewed twice a year, in June and December. Each review has a self-assessment, manager assessment and feedback from three to five peers chosen with the manager. Ratings use a 1 to 5 scale and are calibrated across teams before they are shared. Promotion decisions are made once a year, in the December cycle, and require at least two consecutive reviews at 4 or above.

A manager who gives a rating of 1 or 2 must write a development plan with specific goals and a follow-up date no later than 90 days later. Employees can see their own feedback but peer comments are shown without names. Compensation changes are decided in a separate meeting after calibration and are announced in January. Anyone who disagrees with a rating can ask for a review by their manager's manager within 10 working days.
