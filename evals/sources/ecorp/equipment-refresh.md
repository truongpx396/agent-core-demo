title: Laptop and equipment refresh
---

Company laptops are replaced every 36 months, and a replacement can be requested earlier only when the device is broken or no longer meets the role's needs. Accidental damage is covered once per 12 months; a second incident is charged to the team budget. When someone leaves, all equipment must be returned within 14 days using the prepaid shipping label IT provides.

Employees may keep their old monitor or keyboard at refresh time if IT confirms the item has no company data on it, but laptops and phones must always be returned for secure erasure. Peripherals such as headsets are reordered through the equipment catalogue up to 150 dollars per year without approval. Equipment bought with a personal card and reimbursed remains company property.
